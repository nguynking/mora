Mora is a work chat for Vietnamese teams and their AI coworkers. v3 follows the Grok Bot design language: black, white and grey everywhere, so the only color on screen is the bots themselves.

Reference

What we take from Grok Bot

Reconstructed from xAI's "Designing Grok Bot for a world of persistent agents" and three product screenshots. Full notes and sources: docs/reference/GROK_BOT_DESIGN.md.

Did this help someone delegate, or did it give them one more thing to manage?The question every Grok Bot design decision came back to. Mora uses it as its design test.

Bots, not chats

The sidebar is a roster you scan by avatar. Conversations are pushed down; the bot is what you come back to.

Mora: rooms hold people and bots; bots are recognizable by shape and color.

Monochrome, bots in color

Black, white and grey carry the interface. Avatars are the only saturated color.

Mora: no brand accent at all. Purple only signals a bot at work.

The avatar is the status

Idle, thinking, working, waiting, blocked or done, shown by the avatar's motion rather than badges.

Mora: waiting eyes look up; working bobs and scans.

One mixed transcript

Messages, system events, inline cards and widgets share one timeline. Prose when prose fits, structure when it does not.

Mora: plans are inline cards; approvals are event lines.

Glance, don't supervise

The bot's computer stays its own workspace: an activity signal, then a pinned side panel, then full access.

Mora: the details panel holds work and shared context.

Take things away

Late design work removed window controls, view options and agent metadata.

Mora: no dividers, no per-message chrome until you hover.

Color

Black, white and grey

Both themes side by side. There is no brand accent. Click any row to copy its hex.

The only color: bots

Eight bot colors, assigned from each bot's id. Nothing else in the interface may use them.

Purple means working

The details icon turns purple while a bot in the room is replying or running a task. It is the only signal color.

Readable greys

Secondary text is 4.7:1 on the selected row in light and 5.1:1 in dark. Your black bubble is 19:1.

Never color alone

Every state also has words: "Chờ duyệt", "Đang làm", "Hoàn thành". Red is for errors only.

Bots, people, rooms

A shape, a color, two eyes

Every bot gets a flat shape and two capsule eyes, generated in code so the family stays consistent. Type a name to meet the bot it would become, and switch its state.

State

In the app the seed is the bot's id, so two bots with the same name still look different. Motion stops under reduced motion; the status text carries the state.

Type

The system font, few sizes

SF Pro on Apple, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android. All cover Vietnamese, with nothing to download. Sentence case, no all-caps Vietnamese.

Voice

Plain, first person, one question at a time

Bots call themselves mình, address people by name and never guess anh / chị / em. They restate the request, ask one thing, and show evidence. No emoji.

From v2 to v3

What changed

The v2 palette (jade, turmeric, lacquer on warm paper) is retired. The idea, the voice and the local formats stay.
