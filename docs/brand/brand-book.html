Brand idea

A teammate you can trust with the work

Slack, Teams and Claude Tag now ship shared agents in chat. Mora wins where they do not reach: Zalo-first Vietnamese teams, Vietnamese-first UX and VND billing. The brand has to feel like a capable local colleague, never like a foreign tool with translated strings.

Promise
Làm tiếp, không cần kể lại. Pick up the work. No re-explaining.
Descriptor
Chat làm việc cùng đồng đội AI · Work chat with AI coworkers
For
5–15-person Vietnamese product startups and software studios that coordinate on Zalo. Buyer: the founder or team lead.
Internal line
"Slack with AI coworkers, for teams that live on Zalo." Pitch decks and interviews only, never in the product.
Proof moment
A second teammate continues the agent's task from the room, with no briefing. Every screen should make that easier.

Clear

Says what it will do before it does it. Plans are numbered and short.

Not magical: no "AI-powered" sparkle, no surprises.

Accountable

Shows evidence: the plan, the diff, the test results, who approved.

Not boastful: never claims success without proof.

Local

Vietnamese first, mixed with the English terms devs use. VND, e-invoices, our date format.

Not translated: not an English product wearing Vietnamese strings.

Calm

Quiet paper surfaces, one accent at a time, one looping animation.

Not loud: no gradients, confetti or mascot.

Mora ≈ mở ra

Read aloud, Mora sounds close to mở ra, "to open up". Today the agent's work is locked in one person's session. Mora opens it to the room. Use this reading in onboarding and talks. Do not present it as an etymology.

From v1 to v2

What changed, and why

v1 described "a quiet, familiar messenger" with bots styled like people. The canvas now asks for an AI-native work chat where agents are visibly different and their work sits in its own panel. These are the decisions that moved.

Color

Paper, ink, jade, turmeric, lacquer

Five materials from Vietnamese craft, used strictly as signals. Each accent has exactly one meaning, so a glance at the room tells you who acts next. Hover or focus a swatch to see where it lives; click it to copy the hex for the current theme.

Ngọc · Jade

A person acts or decides

Primary buttons (Duyệt, Gửi), links, focus ring, your own bubbles, selection, success.

Nghệ · Turmeric

The AI is here, working or asking

Agent avatars and messages, the AI label, "Chờ duyệt", "Đang chạy", the AI allowance.

Sơn · Lacquer

Stop, failure, irreversible

The stop button, failed tasks, destructive confirmation. Rare by design.

How much of the screen
Paper and ink · 80% Jade 12% 6%

Turmeric 6%, lacquer 2%. No feature gets its own accent. State always has an icon or text too, never color alone.

    Voice

    Talk like a capable colleague in a work chat

    Short, direct, ordinary Vietnamese, mixed with the English words developers actually say. The agent shows its work instead of its enthusiasm.

    Vietnamese first, mixed where teams mix

    Keep PR, commit, branch, review, deploy, test. Nobody says "yêu cầu kéo".

    Say it before doing it

    The agent states the plan and waits. Nothing runs before a person approves.

    Evidence over excitement

    PR number, tests passed, what did not change. No emoji from the agent.

    Errors that help

    What failed, what is safe, what to do next. No "Có lỗi xảy ra".

    Say this, not that
    The pronoun rule

    Vietnamese pronouns encode age and seniority. An agent cannot know either, so it calls itself mình and addresses people by name. It never guesses anh, chị or em. A team can set its own house style in room settings.

    Motion and icons

    One heartbeat, no fireworks

    The agent's working pulse is the only looping animation, and only while a task runs. Messages arrive without animation. Cards update in place. With reduced motion, the pulse becomes a still dot and the words carry the state.

    Working pulse

    Turmeric dot, 1.6 s, ease-in-out.

    Icons

    Line icons, 1.75 stroke, round caps. 20 px in UI, 16 px in chips. The agent's symbol is the arch, never a robot or sparkle.
